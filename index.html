About

Can one communication radio, sending a 5G-like signal and listening to its own echoes, detect a drone and read the signature of its spinning blades? I am building a ray-traced digital twin with NVIDIA Sionna RT to find out: drone, rotors, ground, buildings and radio, all in simulation.

Notes on the visuals
  • Everything on this page is simulated; no hardware measurements or real flights are shown.
  • Each map is scaled to its own peak unless a shared colour bar is shown, so maps compare shapes, not strengths. Maps have no receiver noise unless the clip or caption says so.
  • Targeted ray launching keeps the rays on the drone; its spectra fall inside the spread between full-sphere runs that differ only in the random seed.
  • Airframe models are simplified meshes built for the simulator; city models are NVIDIA Sionna RT’s built-in scenes; the vegetation clutter is a modelled scenario.
